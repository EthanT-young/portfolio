Rebel Locate

A team project for my machine learning course: locating the UNLV building a photo was taken near from its GPS metadata, and labeling the type of room. My KNN model reached 92% accuracy on the team's 6,000+ image dataset.

Overview

Rebel Locate is a geolocator and room-labeling tool for UNLV. This page covers my implementation.

Approach

We split the main campus between us, and each of us photographed every building in our section. After agreeing on a shared design, we each built our own implementation and compared results across the team.

My implementation:

  • Building (KNN). A K-nearest neighbors classifier trained on the latitude and longitude from each photo's EXIF metadata, with K chosen by 10-fold cross-validation.
  • Room (CNN). Takes only the predicted building's photos and fine-tunes the last layer of a ResNet-18 pre-trained on Places365, a scene-recognition dataset, with one output per room in that building (such as hallway, lounge or stairwell).
  • No near-duplicate leakage. Photos taken within about a meter of each other, such as burst shots, always stay on the same side of every training/testing split, so near-duplicates can't inflate the reported accuracy.
  • Output. The top three buildings and rooms for a photo, each with its probability.

Results

  • 6,000+ images in the team's dataset, covering every building on the main campus.
  • 92% accuracy for my KNN building-proximity model.
